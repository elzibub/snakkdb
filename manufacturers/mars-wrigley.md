Mars' confectionery business. Over the years it has traded as M&M/Mars,
Masterfoods (dropped in 2007; MasterFoods lives on in Australia as Mars'
herbs and sauces brand) and Mars Chocolate. Mars bought Wrigley in 2008, and
the chocolate and Wrigley businesses merged as Mars Wrigley in 2016–17.
