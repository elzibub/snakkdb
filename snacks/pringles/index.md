Saddle-shaped chips pressed from a dough of dried potato and stacked in a
tube. Procter & Gamble created them in 1968, sold them to Kellogg's in 2012,
and they went with Kellogg's snacks business when it was spun off as
Kellanova in 2023. Mars bought Kellanova in December 2025.

Because they are only about 40% potato, P&G argued in a UK tax case that they
weren't potato crisps and so shouldn't carry VAT. The Court of Appeal
disagreed in 2009.
