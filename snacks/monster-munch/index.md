Claw-shaped baked maize snacks, best known in pickled onion. Smiths launched
them in 1977 as *The Prime Monster* and renamed them Monster Munch a year
later. They moved to the Walkers brand in the mid-1990s (sources give 1993–95).
Monster Munch Giants is a separate, bigger version.
