Chocolate buttons in a hard, brightly coloured sugar shell, each printed with
a small "m". Forrest Mars and Bruce Murrie (the two Ms) began making them in
New Jersey in 1941. The shell keeps the chocolate from melting, which made
them popular with US soldiers in the Second World War.
