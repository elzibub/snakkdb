Chewy fruit-flavoured sweets in a crunchy coloured shell, each printed with
an "S". They were first sold in the UK in 1974 (the brand name dates from
1971) and arrived in the US as an import in 1979. Their slogan is "Taste the
rainbow". Wrigley has owned the brand since 2008, and it is now part of Mars
Wrigley.
