Soft, very chewy fruit candy that is something between taffy and gum, but
dissolves completely. Morinaga sold it from 1956 as Chewlets, a candy meant to
chew like gum without needing to be spat out, and relaunched it as Hi-Chew in
1975. It officially came to the US in 2008, and Morinaga now makes it in North
Carolina too. Sold both as sticks and in bags of individually wrapped
"bites".
