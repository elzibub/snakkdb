Light, crunchy wheat sticks made with whole shrimp ground into the dough.
Calbee's long-running slogan is *yamerarenai, tomaranai* ("can't stop, won't
stop"). The *kappa* in the name is the river creature from Japanese folklore.
Nongshim's Saeukkang is
widely said to have been modelled on it.
