Two fingers of shortbread biscuit topped with caramel and coated in milk
chocolate. Launched in the UK in 1967 and in the US in 1979. Much of
mainland Europe knew it as Raider until it was renamed in 1991 ("Raider is
now Twix, nothing else changes"), and the Nordic countries kept the old name
until 2000.
