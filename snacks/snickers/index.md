Peanuts, caramel and nougat in milk chocolate, and one of the best-selling
chocolate bars in the world. Frank Mars named it after a favourite family
horse. In the UK and Ireland it was sold as Marathon until 1990, when Mars
switched to the global name. Marathon has since come back briefly as a retro
limited edition.
