A smooth, sweet, creamy milk chocolate bar in Cadbury purple. It launched in
Birmingham in 1905 with a higher share of milk than earlier bars ("a glass
and a half of full cream milk" in every bar), and by 1914 it was Cadbury's
best seller. Kraft bought Cadbury in 2010, and it is now part of Mondelēz.
In the US, Hershey makes Cadbury Dairy Milk under licence to a different
recipe. It's also hugely popular in India and Australia, which have their
own recipes.
