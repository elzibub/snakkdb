Malted nougat and caramel covered in milk chocolate. Forrest Mars made the
first ones in Slough in 1932 as a British take on his father's American
Milky Way. Sold for decades with the slogan "A Mars a day
helps you work, rest and play". In the US, the nearest equivalent is still
called Milky Way.
