Britain's best-selling crisps. Henry Walker, a Leicester butcher, started
frying potatoes in 1948, when post-war meat rationing left his shop short of
things to sell. PepsiCo bought Walkers in 1989, and the packs now carry the
Lay's logo, but the brand and many of its flavours are its own. Former footballer
Gary Lineker, a Leicester native, has fronted its adverts since 1995.

*Salt & Shake* comes unsalted with a twist of salt to shake in yourself.
*Lightly* is a lower-fat version of the classic crisps.
