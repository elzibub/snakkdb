Dense, sticky, squidgy malt loaf with dried fruit, eaten in slices (often
buttered) or straight from the pack. It's made in Manchester to a 1938
recipe by its Danish-born founder, John Rahbek Sorensen. Lunchbox Loaves are
the same loaf in small, individually wrapped bars.
