Square, individually wrapped fruit chews. They began in Britain as Opal
Fruits ("made to make your mouth water") around 1960. The US version was
called Starburst from its 1967 launch, and in 1998 the British ones took the
global name too. Opal Fruits has since returned briefly as a retro edition.
