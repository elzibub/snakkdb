The American Milky Way: malted nougat and caramel in milk chocolate. Frank
Mars introduced it in 1923 (some sources say 1924), and it was the
company's first big hit. Outside the US it is closest to the Mars bar.
Europe's Milky Way is a different, caramel-free bar.
