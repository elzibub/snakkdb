Baked corn puffs with a bright orange cheese dusting that ends up on your
fingers. Golden Wonder launched them in 1971. Walkers bought the brand in a
deal announced in 2002 and finished in early 2003, when Wotsits took the place
of Cheetos on UK shelves.

The name has since grown into a family: Wotsits Giants and Wotsits Crunchy
are separate snacks that share the brand and flavours.
