Small golden sponge cakes with a cream filling. James Dewar of the Continental
Baking Company created them near Chicago in 1930. The filling was originally
banana, until banana rationing during the Second World War brought in vanilla.

Twinkies disappeared from shops in November 2012 when Hostess went bankrupt
and came back in July 2013 under new owners. The J.M. Smucker Company bought
Hostess Brands in 2023.
