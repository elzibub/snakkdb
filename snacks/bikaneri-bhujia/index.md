Thin, crunchy fried noodles of moth bean and gram flour, spiced and salted.
They are eaten by the handful, with tea, or sprinkled over other dishes.
Hundreds of producers make bhujia in and around Bikaner, Rajasthan, and the
name "Bikaneri Bhujia" has been a protected Geographical Indication since
about 2010. Haldiram's, now one of India's biggest snack companies, started as
a bhujia shop in Bikaner.
