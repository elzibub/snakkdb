Thin fried potato chips, sold in more countries than almost any other snack.
Herman Lay started selling them in Nashville in 1932; his company merged with
Frito in 1961 and joined PepsiCo in 1965.

In Mexico the same chips are sold as Sabritas, a local brand PepsiCo bought
and kept. In the UK and Ireland, Walkers crisps carry the Lay's logo. Local
flavour ranges vary a lot, and PepsiCo regularly runs "Do Us a Flavour"
contests in which customers pick new ones.
