Fruit gummy bears in six colours, and the first gummy bears of all. Hans
Riegel, who started Haribo in Bonn in 1920 (*Ha*ns *Ri*egel *Bo*nn), created
them in 1922 as larger *Tanzbären* ("dancing bears"). They became the smaller
Goldbears later in the century; sources date the change anywhere from 1960
to 1978. The flavours behind each colour differ from country to country.
